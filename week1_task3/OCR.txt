
OCR (Optical Character Recognition) is a technology used to recognize and extract text 
from scanned documents, images, photographs, and image-based PDFs and convert it into 
machine-readable digital text. It is used to make scanned information searchable, 
editable, and easier to store, process, and analyze. In this project, OCR is applied 
to representative scanned documents to extract their text and analyze recognition errors 
caused by factors such as low resolution, skew, equations, tables, diagrams, and handwriting.
